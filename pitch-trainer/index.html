How to practice

Little and often. Ten minutes a day works better than an hour once a week.

Perfect pitch: try to hear each note's colour, not where it sits relative to the last one. Before answering, sing the note back or picture what it feels like. The random run before each note stops you leaning on relative pitch. Once three octaves feel easy, switch it on in Settings.

Intervals: link each interval to the opening of a song you know (switch on reference songs in Settings). Use the songs as a crutch at first, then drop them and listen for each interval's character: bright, tense, open, hollow.

Use what you get wrong. When you miss, play the right answer and your answer back to back. Your stats show which pairs you mix up most.

Levels add one new item each time you get 16 of your last 20 right. Use Custom to drill a specific pair you keep confusing.
